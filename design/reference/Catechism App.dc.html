3cSpec

Long-press

  • Trigger: 400ms long-press anywhere in the paragraph's text column, with a light haptic on fire.
  • Selected paragraph gets a #F6E7DA block behind it — radius 16, inset −10px horizontal / −9px vertical — and its margin number turns #A8562A.
  • Dismiss: tap anywhere else, scroll, or back gesture.
  • Highlight colors reuse the section tints: #F6E7DA, #E9EEDF, #F4E9CE, plus a cool #E6E7EC. The ✕ clears an existing highlight.
  • Applying a highlight keeps the block behind the paragraph permanently in that color.

Which one

3b is the consistent choice — you picked floating pods over full-width bars for the reading chrome, and this keeps the paragraph you're acting on fully visible with no dim.

3a is the safer choice — it has room for labels (better for a first-time user and for accessibility), and it's where a note field, a share-as-image option, or anything you add later will actually fit. Icon-only pods run out of room fast.

Shell

  • Tabs: Trang Chủ · Tìm Kiếm · Đã Lưu · Cài Đặt. Tab tint stays terracotta everywhere — only section screens carry their own tone.
  • Scoped search lives in the section top bar and expands in place; the label under it (“Chỉ tìm trong phần này”) is what keeps it from being confused with the tab.
  • Global search groups results by section with a tinted dot + section rule; each group is capped and gets a “Xem tất cả” row when it overflows.
  • Section tint appears only in the header block and its accents — the body stays on #FBF8F3 so reading is identical across sections.
  • New sections (Kinh Nguyện, Hạnh Các Thánh) need only a name, an icon, and a tint triple to slot into Home + global search.
