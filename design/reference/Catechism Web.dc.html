2eRead this before choosing

2a is the design system's own palette. Picking anything else means the project's bound system no longer decides the look — fine, but it's then on you (and me) to keep it consistent.

2b reads younger and sharper — closest to a modern editorial site. The cooler paper is easier to get right on cheap screens, but it loses the warmth that made the devotional content feel unhurried.

2c is the most obviously Catholic without any ornament — indigo and gold carry the association on their own. Risk: it can tip formal, which cuts against the 18–35 audience.

2d isn't really a competitor — it's the dark mode any of the others need. Shown as its own scheme because if most reading happens at night, it's worth designing dark-first.

Type stays Source Serif 4 + Be Vietnam Pro in all four. Say an id and I'll apply it across the landing page and both readers.

New on web

  • URLs: /giao-ly/27 · /giai-dap/<slug> · /kinh-thanh/ga/1 — every paragraph and verse is linkable, and #28 scrolls to it.
  • Reading measure capped at 600–660px even on wide screens; sidebar 288–300px, right rail 210px.
  • Reading size 18px/1.76 on desktop (up from 17 on the phone); Bible 18px/1.86 for the looser flowing set.
  • Hover: rows tint to #FBF6EE, pills darken to #B0602F, links underline. Focus ring 2px #C67139 offset 2px.
  • Keyboard: J/K paragraph, G jump to number, / focus search.
  • Breakpoints: ≥1200 three columns · 900–1199 drop the right rail · <900 sidebar becomes a drawer, layout as 1d.
  • Verse numbers are superscript 11px #8FA06F inline, 4px trailing space — not a margin column, since verses flow mid-paragraph.

Parked
